The idea

So we turned the web around.

A shopper.A scam filter.A shop window.One card.

Tuttie is your agent, in your phone's wallet. You don't search businesses. Their agents come to you, with offers they signed.

1
You ask onceBy voice or text. Tuttie knows your day, your dog and your budget, and keeps them to herself.

Signed offers come to youOnly verified businesses are woken. Their agents answer with offers they signed, priced by the owner, never by an AI.

Fakes never reach youA seal that doesn't verify is dropped before anyone is asked. Scams aren't blocked one by one; they can't sign.
