2. Apple, Principles of great design, WWDC26 (June 2026)
https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2026/250/
Purpose, agency, simplicity and craft inform the hierarchy and motion. Application: one dominant idea; steady camera while the presenter makes a point; familiar phone and swipe gestures; warm white canvas and restrained colour. These are design choices inferred from guidance, not a claim that Apple has endorsed or tested this pitch.
https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/motion
Short transitions that explain the change; no perpetual handset float or moving glaze.

3. Official TEDx speaker guide
https://storage.ted.com/tedx/manuals/tedx_speaker_guide.pdf
One idea supported by a story, explanation and evidence; simple slides; rehearse aloud and time it. Application: traveller → business reply → owner's rules → human choice. Minimal stage text complements speech. This remains a hackathon product pitch, not an imitation TED talk or an official TED affiliation.

4. Désiron, Endres and Schneider (22 May 2026), primary experimental paper
Is it not too redundant? When signaling overlap reduces extraneous load and enhances retention in a software video tutorial.
https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1795142/full
DOI 10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1795142
In a Photoshop tutorial, integrated visual signals helped retention/transfer and reduced extraneous load. Application: highlight the side-button action and changing price in place; let the phone demonstrate the swipe. Do not generalise this experiment into a claim that this pitch is scientifically guaranteed to persuade or that all redundant text is bad. Findings are about instructional learning, not a three-minute jury pitch.

5. Adobe Digital Insights, 19 August 2026 (primary market research)
https://business.adobe.com/blog/us-consumers-are-embracing-llms-to-make-travel-plans
AI-referred traffic to U.S. travel sites grew 119% year over year in July 2026. Adobe defines this as click-through visits, not autonomous completed bookings. It supports the plausibility of agent-facing discovery, not a claim that agents already book most travel. No percentage appears in the main deck; use this only in Q&A if asked why now. This is more recent and more specifically scoped than the older 539% holiday-2025 figure in the repo storyline.
